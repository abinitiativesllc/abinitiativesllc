## Curated Central American Coffee

**Centam Select** is a veteran-owned coffee brand focused on **small-batch, premium coffees from Central America**.

Founded by **Aaron Byron**, a U.S. Army veteran who lived and worked in Honduras, Centam Select exists to connect discerning coffee drinkers with the growers, roasters, and cooperatives that rarely make it into the U.S. retail market.

We partner with trusted roasters and cooperatives across Honduras and the broader Central American region to bring you:

- **Carefully selected, limited-batch coffees**
- **Origin-driven flavor profiles**
- **Ethically sourced beans from real communities**

This isn’t mass-market coffee.  
It’s coffee with a story, curated with intention.

---

### What We Stand For

- **Origin-first** – We highlight the regions, cooperatives, and families behind each coffee.
- **Veteran-owned values** – Integrity, discipline, and respect for the people in our supply chain.
- **Small-batch curation** – We prioritize quality and character, not volume.
