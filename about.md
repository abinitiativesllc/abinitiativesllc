## Our Story

**Centam Select** was born from lived experience, not theory.

While serving with **Joint Task Force–Bravo** in Honduras, founder **Aaron Byron** spent months working alongside communities across Central America. Early mornings on mountain roads and long days debriefing over strong Honduran coffee became a window into the world behind the beans.

Aaron visited cooperatives and small roasters, watched families hand-sort beans, and saw the pride and precision that go into every harvest. Coffee stopped being a commodity and became a craft — a reflection of land, labor, and legacy.

When he returned home to New York, Aaron wanted to share those flavors and stories with his community. But he didn’t want to become another anonymous importer. He wanted to **curate** the best coffees from the region that changed how he saw the world.

That’s why Centam Select exists.

## What Centam Select Means

**“Centam”** is shorthand for **Central America**, a region rich in culture, resilience, and world-class coffee.

**“Select”** reflects our commitment to:

- Carefully choosing our partners  
- Highlighting small-batch lots  
- Offering coffees that stand out in both quality and character
